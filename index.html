Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about PKHosting VPS, local Pakistani payments, and infrastructure.

Which payment methods can I use in Pakistan without international card blocks?

Unlike foreign hosting providers that require international e-commerce enabled credit cards (which frequently face high declination rates or 10% withholding taxes from local banks), PKHosting lets you pay directly in PKR. We support instant mobile wallets (JazzCash and EasyPaisa), 1Link Raast / Local Online Bank Transfer (IBFT), and all domestic Visa, Mastercard, and UnionPay debit cards.

How does the annual billing discount formula work?

When you choose annual billing, you pay for 10 months and receive 12 full months of service — giving you 2 entire months free of charge (an effective savings of ~16.7%). The monthly equivalent shown on annual plans is calculated strictly as (Monthly Price × 10) / 12. For example, our popular Growth plan drops from Rs 4,800/mo down to Rs 4,000/mo when paid annually.

Where are the VPS servers located and what latency can I expect?

Our infrastructure connects directly to major Pakistani Internet Exchange Points (PKIX in Karachi and Islamabad) with direct peering on PTCL, Nayatel, Transworld, and Jazz. Users inside Pakistan enjoy ultra-low response times between 8ms and 25ms. For overseas audiences, we also provide routing via low-latency Gulf/European gateways.

Can I seamlessly upgrade or downgrade my VPS resources later?

Yes. You can scale CPU, RAM, and NVMe disk space directly from your control panel at any point. Upgrading between Starter, Growth, and Scale plans takes less than two minutes and preserves your IP address and file system intact without data loss.

Do I get full root access and which operating systems can I install?

Every PKHosting VPS instance includes unmetered 100% root SSH access (or Administrator RDP on Windows instances). We offer one-click images for Ubuntu 24.04/22.04 LTS, Debian 12, AlmaLinux 9, Rocky Linux, Arch, and Windows Server 2022, as well as the ability to mount custom ISO files.

Is there a money-back guarantee or trial period?

All new VPS instances are backed by an unconditional 7-day money-back guarantee. If you are not satisfied with network performance or hardware throughput, contact our 24/7 technical team for an immediate full refund back to your original payment method.
