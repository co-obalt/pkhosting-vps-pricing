Frequently asked questions

Answers on local PKR billing, the annual discount formula, server locations, and plan upgrades.

Can I pay in PKR without an international credit card?

Yes. All invoices settle directly in Pakistani Rupees through JazzCash, EasyPaisa, 1Link Raast / IBFT bank transfer, or local Visa, Mastercard, and UnionPay debit cards. You avoid foreign currency conversion fees and bank e-commerce blocks.

How is the annual billing rate calculated?

Annual billing charges 10 months for 12 months of service, giving 2 months free. The displayed monthly equivalent uses (Monthly Price × 10) / 12. For example, the Growth plan drops from Rs 4,800/mo to Rs 4,000/mo when billed at Rs 48,000/year.

Where are the servers hosted and what ping can I expect?

Compute nodes peer directly at Karachi and Islamabad PKIX exchanges with PTCL, Nayatel, Transworld, and Jazz. Connections inside Pakistan see 8 ms to 25 ms round-trip latency, with Gulf and European upstream routes for international visitors.

Can I upgrade my VPS later without changing IP addresses?

Yes. Moving from Starter to Growth or Scale keeps your assigned IPv4 address, /64 IPv6 subnet, and NVMe data intact. Resource resizing finishes from the control panel in under two minutes after a quick reboot.

Which operating systems and root controls are included?

Every KVM instance gives full root SSH access on Linux (Ubuntu 24.04/22.04 LTS, Debian 12, AlmaLinux 9, Rocky Linux) or Administrator RDP on Windows Server 2022. Dedicated Core plans also allow mounting custom ISO images via console.

Is there a refund policy if hardware performance falls short?

New VPS orders include a 7-day money-back window. If network throughput or disk I/O does not meet your workload requirements, open a billing ticket within 7 days for a full refund to your original JazzCash, EasyPaisa, bank, or card account.
